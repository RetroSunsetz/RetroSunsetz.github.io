RETURN & REFUND POLICY

What to do if something's wrong with your order or your detail.

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Shop Products

Because Tire Shine, Wax Shampoo, Rim Cleaner, and the WOW Bundle are liquid chemical products, for hygiene and safety reasons we can only accept a return on a bottle that is unopened, unused, and still sealed, within 3 days of delivery.

If your order arrives damaged, faulty, or not what you ordered, let us know within 24 hours of delivery via WhatsApp (a photo helps) and we'll replace it or refund it at no cost to you.

2. Detailing Services

If you're not happy with a completed detail, let us know within 24 hours so we can take a look and arrange a free touch-up where reasonable.

If you need to cancel or reschedule a booking, please tell us via WhatsApp or phone as early as you can before your appointment.

3. How to Request a Return or Refund

Contact us on WhatsApp or by phone with your order details (and photos, if relevant). We'll confirm next steps with you directly.

4. Refund Method & Timing

Approved refunds are returned using the same method you paid with, or as otherwise agreed with you, and are processed within a reasonable time once approved.

5. Contact Us

Questions about a return or refund? Reach us on WhatsApp or call 35678906.
